OPERATION: THE OTHER MOMMY 🎬❤️

MISSION #002 — The Eastgate Horror Incident

Location: Eastgate Mall
Agents: Agent Mikhail Petrov & Agent Yelizaveta
Classification: 🔴 TOP SECRET
Mission Status: ✅ COMPLETE
Threat Level: 👻 EXTREME — Presidential Bravery Required

🎯 Mission Objectives

  • ☑️ See Agent Yelizaveta and restore the balance of control.
  • ☑️ Do not scream during the horror movie.
  • ☑️ Do not suffer any nightmares.
  • ☑️ Secure Agent Yelizaveta's hand for emotional support.
  • ☑️ Have a wonderful time despite the terrifying conditions.

MISSION RESULT: 5/5 OBJECTIVES COMPLETED. ✅

The Department of Presidential Operations is pleased to announce that both agents successfully completed their second assignment under conditions that can only be described as unnecessarily terrifying.

🚨 Incident #001: Lizzy Pulls a Lebone

The mission started off with Agent Yelizaveta pulling a Lebone.

Intelligence reports confirm that Lizzy arrived slightly later than the agreed time.

While the delay did not threaten the overall mission, it immediately raised concerns within the Presidential Department of Punctuality.

Agent Mikhail, displaying extraordinary patience and restraint, chose not to escalate the situation into an international diplomatic incident.

The matter has been noted.

Official finding: The mission began with a minor scheduling scandal. ⏰😂

🦇 Incident #002: Batman Makes a Questionable Decision

In a development that stunned intelligence officials, Agent Mikhail made the incredibly brave and bold decision to watch a horror movie.

This was particularly surprising considering that he had previously informed Agent Yelizaveta, on multiple occasions, of his strong dislike for horror films.

Investigators have proposed two possible explanations:

Theory A — The Romantic Sacrifice ❤️

Mikhail may have willingly subjected himself to psychological distress simply because he believes that everything is better when experienced alongside Lizzy.

A touching possibility, if true.

Theory B — Operation Hand-Holding Verification 🤝

Alternatively, Mikhail may have deliberately placed himself in a terrifying situation to determine whether Agent Yelizaveta would honour her earlier promise to do whatever was necessary to get him through the movie.

Both theories remain under investigation.

The President has refused to confirm his true motivations.

Official statement: "I'm Batman. I fear nothing."

The intelligence department has requested that the President stop repeating this statement while visibly trembling.

☕ Incident #003: The Coffee Scandal

Before entering the cinema, both agents decided to enjoy some drinks and engage in conversation.

However, the peaceful atmosphere was immediately threatened when Agent Yelizaveta attempted to order a coffee.

A COFFEE.

During a date.

Before a horror movie.

The Department of Beverage Affairs has described this decision as deeply concerning but not entirely unexpected.

Fortunately, the situation was contained, and both agents continued enjoying their drinks.

Conversation flowed effortlessly, covering numerous topics, ranging from everyday nonsense to more serious matters.

During these discussions, Agent Yelizaveta expressed growing concerns regarding Mikael's upcoming birthday party.

Agent Mikhail, recognising the importance of the situation, attempted to reassure her, calm her nerves and ease her worries.

Sources suggest that, for a brief moment, the President abandoned his usual nonsense and demonstrated genuine emotional intelligence.

Government officials have declined to comment on this highly unusual occurrence.

⏰ Incident #004: The Great Beverage Evacuation

As the conversation continued, both agents became so engaged that they almost forgot about the scheduled movie.

A sudden realisation struck:

THE MOVIE WAS ABOUT TO START.

With little time remaining, both agents were forced to finish their drinks at an alarming speed.

A rushed evacuation of the beverage establishment followed.

The agents successfully made their way towards the cinema.

Mikhail was reportedly in no hurry to reach the horror movie.

This allegation has been strongly denied.

👻 Incident #005: Batman Enters Hostile Territory

The cinema lights dimmed.

The movie began.

The atmosphere became increasingly unsettling.

Agent Mikhail remained completely calm, composed and fearless throughout the ordeal.

At least, that is what the official Presidential statement claims.

Independent witnesses have suggested that the President may have experienced several moments of considerable terror.

These reports are entirely false.

Mikhail is Batman.

Batman does not get scared.

Batman simply conducts rapid environmental threat assessments while experiencing temporary increases in heart rate.

Meanwhile, Agent Yelizaveta appeared to be having a suspicious amount of fun watching Mikhail suffer.

Intelligence officials confirm that she took considerable enjoyment in observing the President's reactions.

Mikhail has formally noted this behaviour.

The President does not forget.

And he will most certainly be keeping this information in mind. 🙃

🤝 Incident #006: Operation Hold My Hand

As the terrifying conditions continued, Agent Yelizaveta eventually appeared to experience something resembling sympathy.

In a surprising diplomatic breakthrough, she decided to honour her earlier promise.

Lizzy held Mikael's hand.

What followed has been classified as one of the most successful joint operations in recent Presidential history.

Intelligence reports suggest that both agents rather enjoyed this arrangement.

Mikhail, naturally, accepted the assistance purely for operational reasons.

The hand-holding allegedly prevented the President from running out of the cinema screaming for dear life.

CORRECTION FROM THE PRESIDENTIAL OFFICE:

The President was not preparing to run away screaming.

He was preparing to make a dramatic tactical exit to save the city.

He is Batman, after all.

Nevertheless, Agent Yelizaveta's assistance made the situation considerably more manageable.

The Department of Romance has since opened an investigation into whether holding hands should become a regular activity rather than an emergency horror-movie procedure.

Early findings are overwhelmingly positive. ❤️

🫂 Incident #007: The Hugging Breakthrough

Following the successful completion of the cinema mission, the evening concluded with a lovely hug.

However, this seemingly simple activity produced a significant intelligence breakthrough.

For some time, Agent Mikhail's hugging abilities had been the subject of criticism and unnecessary public scrutiny.

The President has repeatedly maintained that the quality of a hug depends on both participating parties.

Now, new evidence has emerged which may completely clear his name.

📐 The Side-Angle Scandal

Intelligence reports have revealed that Agent Yelizaveta had previously been approaching President Mikael from a sideways angle during hugging operations.

Naturally, Mikael interpreted this positioning as a clear indication that Lizzy wanted a side hug.

Being a respectful gentleman, the President simply responded accordingly.

What else was he supposed to do?

The investigation has therefore raised an extremely important question:

HOW CAN THE PRESIDENT BE ACCUSED OF GIVING SIDE HUGS WHEN THE OTHER PARTY KEEPS APPROACHING FROM THE SIDE?

The Department of Hugging Standards has described this revelation as potentially groundbreaking.

🥋 The Grappling Defence

It is important to note that President Mikael possesses advanced grappling skills.

These abilities have been extensively demonstrated, and Cody Aladeen knows all about them.

In theory, the President was more than capable of using his exceptional grappling expertise to reposition Agent Yelizaveta into the correct hugging angle.

However, Mikael made the responsible decision not to do so.

After all, he had absolutely no intention of looking like a bully or catching a case for wrestling an unsuspecting Lady Lady into the appropriate hugging position.

The President has a reputation to protect.

He is Batman.

Not a public menace.

Therefore, he respected the angle presented to him and delivered what he reasonably believed was the requested side hug.

❤️ The Eastgate Breakthrough

During the latest mission, both agents finally achieved the correct alignment.

The result?

A PROPER, HIGH-QUALITY HUG.

This successful field test has confirmed what the President maintained all along:

He can, in fact, give a very good hug.

The Department of Hugging Standards has therefore issued an important reminder:

A SUCCESSFUL HUG REQUIRES CORRECT ALIGNMENT FROM BOTH AGENTS.

The government also wishes to clarify that advanced grappling techniques are not an approved method of correcting hugging angles.

The President requests that Agent Yelizaveta simply approach from the appropriate direction during future hugging operations.

No wrestling required.

No court cases.

Just a lovely hug. 😂❤️

🕵️‍♂️ Classified Intelligence Discovered

Following a thorough investigation, the Department of Presidential Intelligence has uncovered several important findings.

INTEL #001 — The Hand-Holding Allegations 🤝

Evidence strongly suggests that Agent Yelizaveta enjoys holding Mikael's hand.

Whether she is prepared to admit this publicly and say it with her chest remains another matter entirely.

Her silence has been described as extremely suspicious.

INTEL #002 — The President Is Also Guilty ❤️

Further investigations have confirmed that Mikael also rather enjoys holding Lizzy's hand.

Unlike certain individuals, the President is prepared to acknowledge the evidence.

He has therefore entered a formal recommendation that future hand-holding should not require a national horror emergency.

INTEL #003 — The Horror Movie Loophole 👻

The government has identified a concerning inefficiency in the current hand-holding arrangements.

It should not be necessary for Mikael to risk his life, emotional stability and Batman reputation simply to hold Lizzy's hand.

The Department of Romance recommends more frequent hand-holding under considerably less terrifying conditions.

INTEL #004 — The Great Hugging Misunderstanding 🫂🥋

The investigation has revealed that Agent Yelizaveta's sideways approach was being interpreted by Mikael as a request for a side hug.

The President, despite possessing advanced grappling abilities well known to Cody Aladeen, chose not to forcibly correct her positioning.

Officials have praised his restraint, particularly his desire not to look like a bully or catch a case.

Final finding: Mikael was not incapable of giving proper hugs. He was simply responding to the available body-language intelligence.

The Department of Hugging Standards recommends further practical testing.

Preferably soon.

And preferably with Lizzy approaching from the front.

INTEL #005 — Lizzy Enjoys Presidential Suffering 🙃

Agent Yelizaveta demonstrated an alarming level of amusement while watching the President endure the horror movie.

This behaviour has been permanently recorded.

The President wishes to remind the suspect that he possesses an exceptionally good memory.

No further comment.

INTEL #006 — The Mikael Effect Continues 🧪

Despite the terrifying circumstances, Agent Yelizaveta appeared perfectly comfortable remaining close to Mikael throughout the movie.

Government scientists believe this represents further evidence of the ongoing Mikael Effect.

Agent Yelizaveta is expected to deny these findings.

The government is prepared for this.
